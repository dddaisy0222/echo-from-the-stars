# MIT Future You 问题链审计

体验地址：<https://paths.futureyou.cyborglab.org/>

## 完整问题顺序

### 1. 当前决策

1. What is an important decision you are considering, for the next year? Please ensure it is a decision you're not sure about.
   - Option A: Should I...
   - Option B
2. Which decision are you leaning towards at the moment?
   - Option A
   - Option B
   - One I hadn't thought of

### 2. 最小身份

3. What is your name?
4. Where are you from?
5. How old are you?
6. What are your pronouns?

### 3. 现在的自己

7. Who are the most important people in your life or people you think you will frequently interact with in the future? Include their role or name.
8. What is one thing you are proud of in your life? Describe a professional achievement, project, or moment and why it matters.
9. Describe a significant low point in your life. Include the event, workplace, people, thoughts, emotions, why it was difficult, and what it reveals about your professional growth.
10. Describe a turning point in your life. What happened, when and where, who was involved, and how did it affect your career path or professional development?

### 4. 未来方向

11. What is a life challenge you would like to overcome? How did it develop, how have you addressed it, and why is it important to your professional development?
12. What is a life goal you're working towards? How did you decide on it, how is it developing, and why is it important to you or others?
13. What do you hope your career will be?

### 5. 三十年后的生活

14. Describe your ideal professional lifestyle. What have you accomplished? Are you retired or still working? How many hours? Where? In an organization or your own venture?
15. Describe your ideal financial situation. How do you use money, save, and earn income?
16. Where do you hope to live?
17. What would your daily life look like? Include hobbies and how you spend spare time.

### 6. 视觉连续性

18. Upload or take a current portrait photo.
   - Clear face
   - Well lit
   - Face only
   - No accessories

## 对 Echo 的启示

Future You 通过大量自传材料和年龄渐变照片，建立“未来的我确实延续自现在”的感觉。它的核心是未来自我连续性，不是平行人生模拟。

Echo 不应照搬这 18 个高成本输入。36 小时 Demo 只收集生成因果世界必需的五类信息：

1. 未选择的路；
2. 现实中最终选择的路；
3. 当时的选择逻辑；
4. 无论哪条路都不愿失去的东西；
5. 最想问平行自我的一句话。

身份只保留称呼和当前身份。其余认识应在世界中的选择、物件互动与对话里逐步长出来。

最重要的差异是：Future You 让一个未来的自己回望现在；Echo 让一条未被选择的人生继续生活，再让用户进入其中亲自寻找证据。
